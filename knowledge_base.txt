Summit Tire Supply – Dealer Billing FAQ (demo data)

Billing cycle
- Invoices are generated the day an order ships and emailed to the dealer's billing contact.
- Monthly statements summarizing all open invoices are sent on the 1st of each month.

Payment terms
- Standard terms are Net 30 from the invoice date.
- Dealers in the Gold program receive Net 45 terms.
- A 2% early-payment discount applies if paid within 10 days of the invoice date.
- Payments are accepted through the dealer portal by ACH or card. Agents never take payment details by phone.
- Payments can take up to 2 business days to post.

Late payments
- A 1.5% monthly late fee applies to balances more than 15 days past due.
- Accounts more than 60 days past due are placed on credit hold, which pauses new orders until the balance is paid.

Pricing and quotes
- Invoice prices come from the dealer's approved quote at the time of order.
- Prices for raw-material-sensitive products can be adjusted monthly; adjustments are announced 30 days in advance.
- If an invoice price doesn't match the approved quote, the dealer can open a pricing dispute.

Credits and returns
- Approved returns are credited within 5 business days of the return being received at the warehouse.
- Warranty credits are issued after the claim is approved, usually within 10 business days.
- Credits appear on the next monthly statement and can be applied to any open invoice.

Disputes
- Dealers can dispute any invoice line within 60 days of the invoice date.
- Opening a dispute pauses late fees on the disputed amount while it is reviewed.
- Disputes are reviewed by the billing team within 5 business days, and the dealer receives an email with the outcome.

Contact and escalation
- Billing specialists are available Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. Central.
- Issues over $5,000 or involving a credit hold are routed to a senior billing specialist.
